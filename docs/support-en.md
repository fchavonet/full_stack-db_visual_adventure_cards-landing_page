## About DBVAC

DBVAC grew out of a passion for Dragon Ball Visual Adventure cards and a simple need: a practical tool to keep track of your collection, find your missing cards and manage your duplicates.

DBVAC is not an official app. It is not affiliated with [Bandai](https://www.bandai.co.jp/en/) or the rights holders of the Dragon Ball franchise.

## Intellectual property

The card reproductions displayed in DBVAC help collectors identify cards and manage their collections.

The illustrations, characters, trademarks and logos shown on the cards belong to their respective owners. Their presence in the app does not imply any affiliation with DBVAC.
