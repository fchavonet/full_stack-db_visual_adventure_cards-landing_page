## Your privacy

You can use DBVAC without creating an account in the app. iCloud backup is optional and uses your Apple Account.

DBVAC does not sell your data or use it for advertising or direct marketing.

[Fabien Chavonet](https://fchavonet.dev), the developer of DBVAC, is your contact for privacy questions and the data controller for inquiries received at the contact email address.

## Your collection

Your collection is stored on your device, including the cards you own, their quantities, their condition and your favorites.

The app also keeps local backup copies to help recover your collection if its file becomes corrupted. Older backup copies are replaced as you make changes.

Deleting the app from your device removes its local data. This does not delete files you have exported or copies held in iCloud or in device backups.

## Catalog and images

DBVAC connects to GitHub Pages to update the catalog and download card images. GitHub records the IP address used for these connections for security purposes.

Your collection’s quantities, card conditions and favorites are not sent to GitHub. GitHub’s privacy statement describes how it processes and retains technical data.

You can remove a part’s downloaded images in Settings → Offline images. Your collection will not change.

[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## iCloud Backup

If you enable iCloud backup, DBVAC automatically saves a copy of your collection in private iCloud storage associated with your Apple Account.

When you enable it, if the app does not yet contain any collection data and a backup is available, DBVAC restores it automatically.

Apple provides storage for this copy. To learn more about how Apple processes data, see its [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

You can turn off iCloud backup in DBVAC’s settings. This stops new backups but does not delete the existing copy.

To delete that copy, first turn off backup in DBVAC, then delete the app’s data in your device’s iCloud storage settings.

This option is separate from your device’s overall backup, which depends on your Apple settings.

## Import and export

Exporting creates a CSV file containing your collection information. You choose where to save it or who to share it with. DBVAC does not delete copies you save or share.

When importing a file, the app asks for confirmation before replacing your current collection.
